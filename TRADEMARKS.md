# Trademarks and artwork

## Our base

OldNet OS is **based on Bazzite**. We say so to describe where the system comes
from, nothing more. OldNet OS does not use Bazzite's name as its own product
name, and it ships none of Bazzite's logos, artwork or branding. Bazzite,
Universal Blue and Fedora are the marks of their respective owners. OldNet OS is
not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

## Original artwork only

Everything visual or audible in OldNet OS is original work: logos, wallpapers
(including "Bliss Neon"), icons, cursors, sounds, boot and login screens. The
look takes loose inspiration from early-2000s desktops and from the cyberpunk
genre in general. It copies neither.

OldNet OS does **not** include, and contributions must not add:

- artwork, logos, fonts, names or other assets from CD Projekt or its games;
- Microsoft assets such as the Bliss photograph, Windows sounds, logos,
  Space Cadet Pinball or any other Windows artwork;
- Bazzite, Universal Blue or Fedora logos or artwork.

## The OldNet name and logo

The Apache-2.0 license covers the code. It grants no rights to the "OldNet OS"
name or logo (License section 6). Forks are welcome, but a fork that changes the
system must use a different name and logo.
